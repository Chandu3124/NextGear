Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how NextGear uses cookies and similar technologies to improve website performance, user experience, and basic analytics.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They help remember preferences and support website functionality.

2. How We Use Cookies

We may use cookies to understand website traffic, improve page performance, remember user preferences, and maintain basic functionality.

3. Types of Cookies

Our website may use essential cookies, performance cookies, and limited analytics cookies to improve the overall browsing experience.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Some parts of the website may not work properly if cookies are turned off.

5. Third-Party Cookies

Some external tools or embedded services may set their own cookies. These are governed by the policies of those providers.

6. Policy Updates

We may revise this Cookie Policy when website tools or cookie usage practices change. Updates will be shown on this page.
