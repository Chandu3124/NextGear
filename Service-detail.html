Theory Test Preparation

Simple support to strengthen your understanding of rules, signs, and safe driving basics.

What is the theory test?

It is a guided practice module that improves your understanding of road signs, traffic rules, and responsible driving behavior.

How does it work?

Learners answer structured questions and receive instant feedback after each attempt.

What happens after completion?

You can review mistakes, improve weak areas, and prepare better for the official test.
