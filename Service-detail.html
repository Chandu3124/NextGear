Theory Test Preparation

A simple question-and-answer format to explain how theory preparation works.

What is the theory test?

It is a practice-based learning module designed to test your knowledge of road signs, traffic rules, responsibilities, and safe driving behavior.

How does it work?

Learners answer interactive questions in a structured format and receive instant feedback after each attempt.

What happens after completion?

You can review your results, understand where mistakes happened, and improve weak areas before taking the official theory examination.
